Source: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, Staff Report on Equity and Options Market Structure Conditions in Early 2021, October 14, 2021.
Official URL: https://www.sec.gov/files/staff-report-equity-options-market-struction-conditions-early-2021.pdf
L1: GME short interest (as a percent of float) in January 2021 reached 122.97%.
L2: By January 27, GME closed at a high of $347.51 per share; the following day, share prices jumped further to an intraday high of $483.00.
L3: From January 13-29, an average of approximately 100 million GME shares traded per day, an increase of over 1,400% from the 2020 average.
L4: By January 27, the number of unique accounts trading GME on a given day increased from less than 10,000 at the beginning of the month to nearly 900,000.
L5: SEC staff concluded that positive sentiment, not buying-to-cover, sustained the weeks-long price appreciation and did not find evidence of a gamma squeeze in GME during January 2021.
L6: On January 27, NSCC made intraday margin calls from 36 clearing members totaling $6.9 billion, bringing the total required margin across all members to $25.5 billion.
L7: Some broker-dealers restricted activity in reaction to margin calls and capital charges imposed by NSCC; NSCC did not instruct firms to stop trading individual symbols.
