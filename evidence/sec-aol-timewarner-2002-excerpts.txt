Source: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission complaint and AOL Time Warner 2002 Form 10-K.
Official URLs: https://www.sec.gov/file/complaint-matter-35 ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1105705/000095014403004064/g81332e10vk.htm
L1: AOL and Time Warner announced a proposed merger in January 2000 with an implied value of approximately $200 billion, then the largest merger in U.S. history.
L2: The merger was completed on January 11, 2001; each outstanding Time Warner equity security was converted into 1.5 units of an equivalent AOL Time Warner equity security.
L3: In the first quarter of 2002, AOL Time Warner recorded a $54.199 billion non-cash pretax goodwill impairment, substantially all generated by the merger.
L4: In the fourth quarter of 2002, the company recorded an additional $45.538 billion impairment of goodwill and other intangible assets.
L5: The additional charge included $33.489 billion for AOL, $10.550 billion for Cable, $646 million for Music goodwill, and $853 million for Music brands and trademarks.
L6: The company stated that the first charge mainly reflected the decline in its stock price since the merger was announced and valued for accounting purposes in January 2000.
